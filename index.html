💡 macOS Video Recording Guide

  • 1
    Upload your video. The app automatically detects the native resolution (e.g., 1080p, 4K) to record at its original size.
  • 2
    Toggle "Mix Microphone Audio" if you want to narrate over the video sound.
  • 3
    Click Start Recording. Playback starts immediately. No window or screen sharing selection prompt is required!
  • 4
    The engine captures the raw media streams directly. Player controls, background UI, and mouse cursors are never recorded. Press ESC to stop early.
